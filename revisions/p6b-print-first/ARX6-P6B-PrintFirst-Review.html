What is built in this checkpoint

The upper/forearm tube spans are reinforced from 2.5 to 6 mm nominal wall. The long upper frame becomes two pieces plus an 80 mm lap coupler with through-sleeve bolt passages. Coarser module-1.2 gripper pinion/rack trials replace the assumption that the original module-0.3 teeth can simply be printed.

Checks and boundaries

Six watertight, consistently wound, positive-volume single-component STL files; six valid single-solid STEP reimports; nominal three-piece upper-splice interference checked. The structural study is a 36-case uniform-beam surrogate, not complete CAD FEM or actual TCP-error prediction. Full-arm integration, joint compliance, print qualification, creep, thermal behavior and motor/reduction sizing remain open.

Part-by-part manufacturing disposition

The original material is not treated as a reason to keep a whole custom part in metal. Printed carriers may retain small metal journals, preload sleeves, heat-transfer plates or inserts. Purchased reducer envelopes are flagged for a true printed transmission redesign, not relabeled as printable gear internals.
